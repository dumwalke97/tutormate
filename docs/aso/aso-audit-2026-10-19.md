## Preview video brief

Pillar 6 is 20 of the 155 points and currently returns zero. No other single asset on the
listing recovers that much.

### Hard constraints

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 15–30 seconds. Apple rejects anything outside this. |
| Orientation | Portrait, matching the screenshot set |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 for the 6.9" iPhone slot. Confirm against App Store Connect's media manager at upload time, since Apple revises these. |
| Format | H.264 or Apple ProRes 422 HQ, .mov / .mp4 / .m4v, 30 fps |
| Audio | **Autoplay is muted.** Every word that matters must be on screen. Treat audio as decoration. |
| Poster frame | Chosen by you in ASC. Pick the split worksheet-to-quiz frame, never a logo or a loading state. |
| Content rule | Apple requires footage captured in-app. No live action, no actors, no marketing animation beyond titles and transitions. |

### Storyboard

Each beat names what fills the screen and what text overlays it. Total 25 seconds.

| # | Time | On screen | Overlay text | Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.5s | A hand holding a real math worksheet, shot through the app's camera viewfinder | "Scan any homework" | 6.2, 6.3 |
| 2 | 1.5–4.0s | Shutter fires; the captured worksheet snaps into the upload card | "Snap a photo" | 6.2 |
| 3 | 4.0–6.5s | Generate Quiz button taps; a brief shimmer; question 1 of 10 renders | "AI builds your quiz in seconds" | 6.2, 6.3 |
| 4 | 6.5–11.0s | A question answered; the correct option fills green with the Correct pill | "Instant feedback, every question" | — |
| 5 | 11.0–15.0s | The Assignment Feedback screen scrolls, showing student answer, correct answer and explanation | "Check finished homework, with explanations" | 6.3 |
| 6 | 15.0–18.5s | Quiz Complete, 10 / 10 | "Know it before the test" | — |
| 7 | 18.5–21.5s | Folders view; a worksheet drops into Biology Unit 3 | "Everything saved and synced" | — |
| 8 | 21.5–25.0s | Hold on the app icon over the navy field | "Start free. Your first 10 quizzes are on us." | 6.4 |

### How each criterion is satisfied

- **6.1 Video present.** One video on the 6.9" slot clears it. You may upload up to three.
- **6.2 Value clear in 5 seconds muted.** Beats 1 through 3 land the entire premise, worksheet to quiz, by second 6. Nothing opens on a logo, a splash or a loading spinner.
- **6.3 Reinforces title and subtitle keywords.** The overlays carry "scan", "homework", "quiz" and "AI", four tokens from the live title and subtitle. The rubric needs two.
- **6.4 Closing CTA.** Beat 8 is an explicit offer, not a feature shot or an end card.

### Two things that will sink it

1. **Do not open on the current home screen.** It shows a Log In / Sign Up bar and an empty topic field. That is the same mistake as screenshot slot 1: the first frame reads as a signup wall rather than a product.
2. **Do not rely on voiceover.** Muted autoplay is the default on both the product page and in search results. A video whose value lives in narration scores zero on 6.2 regardless of how good the narration is.

### Localization note

A video uploaded to the US English localization serves only that one. If you add locales later,
each needs its own upload or it falls back to screenshots.

## Off-rubric findings (added 2026-10-19)

The 155-point rubric measures listing quality. These are not listing problems, and three of
them outrank everything in the opportunities table.

### 1. The listing required iOS 26.2 — CRITICAL, now fixed in the working tree

**Corrected 2026-10-19.** My first diagnosis was wrong about the cause, though right about the
symptom. I compared the listing against `tutormate/ios/App`, a Capacitor project that targets
iOS 15.0. That project is not what ships.

The App Store build comes from a **separate repository**, `~/Documents/GitHub/TutorMate-ios`,
a native SwiftUI app. Its `PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER` is `com.tutormateai.tutormate`, matching
App Store id 6793738685. There was no mismatch and no mystery build-environment override: that
project simply had `IPHONEOS_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET = 26.2` set in both configurations.

The gate itself was entirely real. "Requires iOS 26.2 or later" excluded every device below
that version from installing, ahead of anything metadata could influence.

**Status (updated 2026-10-20):** resolved by the developer at **iOS 17.0**, not the 16.0 first
proposed, and committed to `TutorMate-ios` `main` as `0dff7bb` "Record the shipped state: v1.2
build 11, iOS 17 minimum".

That commit also revealed the underlying cause of the confusion: v1.1 and v1.2 were both
archived and uploaded straight from an uncommitted working tree, so the source of each shipped
build existed only on disk. The repository never described what was in the store.

**Not yet live.** As of 2026-10-20 the App Store still serves v1.1 with `minimumOsVersion 26.2`.
v1.2 build 11 is uploaded but unreleased, so the gate stays closed until that version goes live.
Watch the listing rather than the repo to confirm.

**Resolved.** The unrelated uncommitted work in that tree (`Info.plist`, `FirebaseManager.swift`,
`CustomNavigationBar.swift`, and the version bumps in `project.pbxproj`) was committed by the
developer across `0dff7bb` and `b2e3f59`.

**Process note worth keeping.** Shipping from an uncommitted working tree is what made this
finding hard to diagnose and would have made a regression impossible to bisect. Tag each release
commit, or at minimum commit before archiving.

**Note on repo scope.** Everything else in this audit concerns the listing, which is correct
regardless of which repo builds it. But any code-level recommendation here (the in-app review
prompt, the screenshot generator) belongs in `TutorMate-ios`, not in this repository.

### 2. No Smart App Banner on tutormate.ai

No `apple-itunes-app` meta tag anywhere in `www/`. You run a working web version of this
product. Every web visitor on an iPhone is a pre-qualified install candidate, and the banner
is one meta tag:

```html
<meta name="apple-itunes-app" content="app-id=6793738685">
```

Apple attributes installs through it, so it also shows up in App Analytics as a distinct source.

### 3. Only one localization

The listing serves a single locale. Each additional localization adds its own 30-character
title, 30-character subtitle and 100-character keyword field, and Apple indexes keywords from
secondary locales inside the primary market's search results. Adding Spanish alone roughly
doubles indexed surface in the US. This is the cheapest ranking surface Apple offers and the
rubric does not score it at all.

### 4. Seller name reads "DUSTIN MICHAEL WALKER"

On a listing aimed at parents of school-age children, an all-caps personal legal name is a
weaker trust signal than a company name. If Walker Intelligence Group is the entity, switching
the seller display name in App Store Connect costs nothing.

### 5. Product Page Optimization is unused

Apple ships free native A/B testing: up to three treatments against a control, real traffic
splits, statistical significance reported in App Store Connect. The keyword testing lab above
describes sequential releases because that is what the rubric assumes. For icon and screenshot
tests, PPO is strictly better and does not need a release.

### 6. No iPad and no "Designed for iPhone" on Mac

The listing says "Only for iPhone" and "Not verified for macOS". Homework happens on iPads.
Enabling the Mac catalyst checkbox alone adds a storefront at zero marginal cost.

### On the video specifically

It is 20 of the 155 points, which makes it look like the biggest item on the board. For
installs it is not, and the honest case against doing it now:

- A/B test data on preview videos is genuinely mixed outside games. For utility and
  productivity apps, videos frequently test neutral or negative, because the video replaces
  the first screenshot in the autoplaying slot and adds a delay before the user sees anything.
- A mediocre video is worse than none, since it displaces your strongest asset.
- It is the highest-effort item in the whole audit.
- At current traffic there is almost nothing to convert. Conversion work pays off in
  proportion to impressions, and impressions are gated by items 1 through 4.

Do it, but after the traffic exists, and validate it with PPO rather than shipping it blind.
