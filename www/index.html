Built to teach, not to hand over answers

TutorMate is deliberately not an answer machine. You do the work first; TutorMate checks it afterwards. When you upload a finished assignment, it marks what's right, what's off, and explains the reasoning behind each one, so you understand the method rather than copying a result. When you want to practice, it builds a quiz from your notes or any topic you type in. That design choice is why it is a study partner rather than a shortcut.

Frequently asked questions

Is TutorMate a cheating app?

No. TutorMate is built around checking work you have already done, not producing answers for you to copy. You complete the assignment, photograph it, and TutorMate marks each answer right or wrong and explains the reasoning. The quiz generator works the same way: it creates practice questions from your material so you can test yourself. Teachers and parents generally object to tools that supply finished answers on demand; TutorMate's workflow requires you to attempt the work first.

How much does TutorMate cost?

TutorMate costs $4.95 per month or $49 per year. Your first 10 assignment checks and quizzes are free, with no credit card required to start. The price is the same whether you subscribe on the web or in the iOS app, and you can cancel at any time. The annual plan works out to roughly $4.08 per month.

Can TutorMate check handwritten homework?

Yes. TutorMate reads handwritten answers as well as printed worksheets. Photograph the page you filled in by hand and it will work through the answers one by one. Clear, well-lit photos of the whole page give the most reliable results, and dense or heavily annotated worksheets take longer to process than simple ones.

What devices does TutorMate work on?

TutorMate runs in any modern web browser at tutormate.ai and as a native iPhone and iPad app on the App Store. Your folders and saved work sync across both, so you can photograph a worksheet on your phone and review it later on a laptop. There is no Android app at present; Android users can use the web version.

How is TutorMate different from Photomath, Socratic, Gauth, and Chegg?

Most homework apps are solvers: you photograph a question you have not answered and they return the worked solution. TutorMate inverts that. It is a checker, so you photograph a question you have already answered and it tells you whether you got it right and why. Photomath and Gauth focus on solving math problems step by step. Socratic surfaces explanations and resources for a question. Chegg provides textbook solutions and expert answers at a substantially higher subscription price. TutorMate also generates practice quizzes from your own material, which solver apps generally do not.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Images and text you submit are sent over an encrypted connection to TutorMate's server and processed by Google's Gemini API to produce the feedback or quiz. Work you save to folders is stored in Google Firebase and kept until you delete it. Your content is never used for advertising and is never sold. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it from the app menu. The privacy policy sets this out in full.
